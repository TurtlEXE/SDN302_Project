    1. `User` (`users`): email (unique, lowercase), fullName, phoneNumber, role (`CUSTOMER`, `OWNER`, `ADMIN`), isLocked, tokenVersion.
    2. `AuthToken` (`auth_tokens`): tokenHash (unique), type (`VERIFY_EMAIL`, `ACTIVATE_OWNER`, `RESET_PASSWORD`), expiresAt (TTL 7 ngày).
    3. `AuthSession` (`auth_sessions`): userId, absoluteExpiresAt (TTL 1 ngày sau hết hạn), revokedAt, revokedReason.
    4. `RefreshToken` (`refresh_tokens`): tokenHash (unique), expiresAt (TTL 7 ngày), sessionId, usedAt, revokedAt, replacedByTokenId.
    5. `Sport` (`sports`): name, normalizedName (unique), isActive.
    6. `Facility` (`facilities`): ownerId, name, address, province, openTime/closeTime, status (`DRAFT`, `PUBLISHED`, `PAUSED`), isLockedByAdmin, images (max 10), text index `{ name: "text", address: "text" }`.
    7. `FacilitySport` (`facility_sports`): facilityId + sportId (unique compound), slotStepMinutes enum `[30, 60, 90]`, configVersion.
    8. `Court` (`courts`): facilitySportId + name (unique compound), pricePerHourVnd (min: 1), priceVersion, isActive.
    9. `CourtDaySchedule` (`court_day_schedules`): courtId + bookingDate (unique compound), scheduleVersion (atomic increment).
    10. `CourtBlock` (`court_blocks`): courtId, bookingDate, startTime/endTime, startMinutes/endMinutes, reason, isActive.
    11. `BookingRequest` (`booking_requests`): requestNumber (unique), createdBy + idempotencyKey (unique compound), holdExpiresAt, payloadFingerprint.
    12. `Booking` (`bookings`): bookingCode (unique), courtId + bookingDate + status + startMinutes + endMinutes (query overlap), durationMinutes, pricePerHourVnd, totalPriceVnd, allocatedAmountVnd, status, paymentStatus, snapshot, cancellation, completion, statusVersion.
    13. `PaymentOrder` (`payment_orders`): bookingRequestId (unique), vnpayTxnRef (unique), expectedAmountVnd, status, expiresAt.
    14. `PaymentTransaction` (`payment_transactions`): vnpayTransactionNo (unique partial), vnpayTxnRef, amountVnd, isAccepted, rawCallbackData.
    15. `RefundCase` (`refund_cases`): refundCaseCode (unique), paymentTransactionId, refundAmountVnd, status (`PENDING`, `REFUNDED`).
    16. `EmailOutbox` (`email_outbox`): eventKey (unique), eventType, recipientEmail, templateName, templateData, status, retryCount, nextRetryAt.
    17. `ActionLog` (`action_logs`): actorId, actorRole, targetType, targetId, action, previousState, newState, ipAddress.

    - Khi có lỗi cố ý trong transaction: toàn bộ document chèn vào User, Facility, BookingRequest đều bị rollback 100%, không còn tồn tại trên DB.

    - Khi gọi lồng `withTransaction(fn, existingSession)`: tự động tái sử dụng `existingSession` của transaction ngoài, không gây lỗi lồng transaction của MongoDB.
